# Preserve Connected Meaning in Plain Writing

Chose reader understanding over rules that always split sentences or remove
repetition. A cause, contrast, or qualification can belong to one coherent
thought. Parallel definitions and stable terms can make that thought easier
to follow. Neither longer sentences nor repetition becomes a goal.

Issue #267 authorizes this revision to the rules identified in
[the exposition assessment](https://github.com/freeasinbird/free-skills/blob/7b9cfc5da23120c74c373deb6e3082dd8cd35ad9/devlog/2026-09-23-0030-prose-revision-calibration.md).
It preserves the [original skill boundary](2026-08-28-0801-write-plainly-skill.md):
general guidance in the entry point and substantial examples loaded on demand.
Calibration interviews and manuscript passes remain in refine-exposition.

Faithfulness includes causal steps, conceptual scope, qualifications, and
whose actions, beliefs, or feelings the text describes. Explaining a useful
consequence must stay within supplied context, preserving the
[evidence decision](2026-09-04-2340-writing-evidence.md). Smooth prose cannot
offset an invented fact or a changed relationship. The paired consequence
cases test both explaining supplied significance and refusing to invent it.

The plan-work-unit fallback changes because its preference for splitting
joined clauses would conflict with the revised skill. This follows the
[fallback note's revisit condition](2026-09-01-1445-plan-work-unit-plain-writing.md).
Its shorter rules retain connected meaning, scope, perspective, supplied
consequences, and useful parallel wording. They omit the full tone guidance,
teaching examples, and revision checks because planning needs a compact
fallback. The loading gate and exact template headings remain intact.

Rejected appending new rules alongside the old absolutes: an agent would
still have to choose between conflicting instructions. Also rejected making
naturalness mean conversational or emotional writing in every setting.
Acceptable prose can stay unchanged, and a short reply can stay short.

Revisit when ordinary replies begin a revision process, when connected prose
loses necessary boundaries, or when repeated evaluations expose a persistent
gap between the full skill and its planning fallback.
